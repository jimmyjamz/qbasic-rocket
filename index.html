Rocket Planet Loop

Launch, guide the rocket with the mouse, land on a planet, step out as an astronaut, walk the terrain, climb back in, and fly to the next world.

Move the mouse during flight to guide the rocket. After landing, press E to exit.

Planet Launchpad
Mode Rocket
Altitude 0 ft
Throttle Idle
Loop Waiting 
FOR planet = 1 TO 4
  LAUNCH_ROCKET
  LAND_ON planet
  astronaut = EXIT_ROCKET()
  WHILE astronaut.exploring
    WALK_AROUND
  WEND
  ENTER_ROCKET
NEXT planet
